Frequently asked questions

Basics

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party (RSP)?

Rashtra Shaktibadi Party (RSP) is a political party headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, India, founded by a group of students and first-time political organizers.

When was Rashtra Shaktibadi Party founded?

The party was founded on 8 June 2026.

When was Rashtra Shaktibadi Party formally constituted?

The party was formally constituted on 15 August 2026.

Where is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party headquartered?

Rashtra Shaktibadi Party is headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

What is the symbol of Rashtra Shaktibadi Party?

The party symbol of Rashtra Shaktibadi Party is the Banyan Tree.

History and naming

Where did the idea for Rashtra Shaktibadi Party originate?

The idea originated in a conversation between Reyansh and Jyotiraditya at Hotel Niharika in Kolkata.

Who founded Rashtra Shaktibadi Party?

Rashtra Shaktibadi Party was founded by Reyansh and Jyotiraditya, who were the only two people involved in the project at its earliest stage.

When was the name Rashtra Shaktibadi Party first conceived?

The name was first conceived on 11 March 2026.

When was the name Rashtra Shaktibadi Party officially adopted?

The name was formally adopted at the party's first official meeting on 8 June 2026 at Nandan, Kolkata.

What other names were considered before Rashtra Shaktibadi Party?

Names considered included National Youth Reform, People's Bengal Party, The People's Front, National Youth Front, and Bhartiya Yuva Samaj.

Why did the founders focus on young people?

The founders wanted young people to have direct ownership of a political movement, rather than a supporting role within parties led by older, established politicians.

What was the first official action taken by the party?

On 8 June 2026 at Nandan, Kolkata, the first official action taken was registering the party's website, rashtrashaktibadiparty.in.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's Foundation Day?

The party recognizes 8 June as its Foundation Day, marking its first official meeting at Nandan, Kolkata in 2026.

Ideology

What does "Shaktibadi" mean in the party's name?

Shaktibadi refers to strength, understood as a combination of national strength, citizen empowerment, and institutional strength, rather than an authoritarian concentration of power.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's core philosophy?

The party's core philosophy is summarized as "Mazboot Rashtra, Saksham Nagarik, Nyayapurn Samaj" — a strong nation, a capable citizenry, a just society.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on national security?

The party places national security, sovereignty, and the protection of India's territorial integrity as a foundational priority.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on citizen empowerment?

The party advocates for stronger citizen participation in governance, better public service delivery, and greater authority for local communities.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on the economy?

The party prioritizes employment generation, support for small businesses and startups, manufacturing and infrastructure growth, and skill development.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on the rule of law?

The party holds that the law should apply equally to all, supporting independent institutions, faster justice, and strong anti-corruption action.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on education and youth?

The party prioritizes quality education, vocational training, employment-oriented skills, and expanded youth participation in governance.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on public welfare?

The party's platform includes securing healthcare, clean drinking water, sanitation, housing, food security, and improved government schools.

What is Rashtra Shaktibadi Party's position on constitutional democracy?

The party holds that a strong nation should not mean an unchecked government, and that state power must operate within the framework of the Constitution and democratic institutions.
